## Changes in this artifact

The source PNG images and masks were copied byte for byte. The folder layout was normalized, and `labels.csv`, `metadata.jsonl`, `classes.json`, and a dataset manifest were added. The official 561-image training and 140-image validation split was preserved. No test split was added.

## Rights and limits

The Mendeley v1 snapshot identifies CC BY 4.0 and states that additional permission may be required for content within the dataset identified as belonging to third parties. The dataset page describes the images as captured in rural areas of Santa Catarina, Brazil. A representative sample of 12 images was reviewed; this is not a complete third-party rights or privacy audit.

Use the dataset to explore pixel-level road-surface labeling in an educational setting. It is geographically specific and does not represent Japanese roads. Do not use model predictions to determine whether a real road is safe, passable, or in need of repair.
