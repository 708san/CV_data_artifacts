# Strawberry-DS v1 rights review

- Source: [Mendeley Data v1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/z6dtfdpzz8/1), DOI `10.17632/z6dtfdpzz8.1`.
- Mendeley record license: CC BY 4.0. Any permitted sharing requires appropriate attribution, a link to the license, and notice of changes.
- The archive contains `README.roboflow.txt` and `data.yaml`; both point to Roboflow workspace `fym-no3zr/strawberry-2szl9`, version 4, and `data.yaml` declares `license: Private`.
- Mendeley’s FAQ says it does not validate or curate dataset content. The Mendeley CC BY label therefore does not by itself resolve the apparent upstream licensing conflict.
- Decision: hold mirroring and redistribution pending clarification that the contributors had rights to relicense the Roboflow-derived images and annotations. No image artifact was generated.
- Required attribution if rights are clarified: Nashwa El-Bendary; Esraa Elhariri (2022), “Strawberry-DS”, Mendeley Data, V1, DOI: 10.17632/z6dtfdpzz8.1.
