## Changes and scope

CV_data included the `Compress Dataset` image variant only and excluded the separate `Raw Dataset` duplicate set. Category folders were normalized, image pixels were preserved, and deterministic class-stratified train/validation/test splits (seed 42) were added. The compressed variant contains 5,136 images; the source page's per-class totals match 5,135 images in the raw variant, while the compressed RoundAbout folder has one additional image.

A sample of one image per class was visually reviewed; this is not a manual review of every image. Some photos may show public-road background activity. The images represent Bangladeshi signs and settings, not all road systems. Use for classroom learning and sign-literacy prototypes, not real traffic, navigation, or safety decisions. This preparation is not endorsed by the dataset authors.
