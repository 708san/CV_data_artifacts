## Intended educational use and limits

家庭や学食で先に現物確認する青果の候補を考え、食品ロス削減を補助する. 14,084 unique 512x512 RGB images across 24 author-labeled classes. Parenthetical days are source labels, not measured remaining life. Predictions do not determine edibility or food safety. Contact sheet visually sampled across all classes.
