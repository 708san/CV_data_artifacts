## Intended educational use and limits

火・煙などの教材画像を分類し、周囲の人による確認手順を考える. Author categories are Fire, Smoke, Safe_Fire, Artificial_Fire. “Safe” is only the source label and does not indicate real-world safety. Never use for alarm, evacuation, or emergency decisions. 48 images visually sampled.
