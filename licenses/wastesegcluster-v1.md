## Intended educational use and limits

水辺に見えるごみを検出して清掃場所の確認・活動計画を補助する. Collected around Dhaka, Bangladesh. The package provides bounding boxes although source includes polygons. One image has no source annotations; empty labels mean no boxes supplied, not a confirmed clean scene. Not a measure of water quality or health risk. 50 images visually sampled.
