# HGM-4 v8 rights and inspection review

- Source: [Mendeley Data v8](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/jzy8zngkbg/8), DOI `10.17632/jzy8zngkbg.8`.
- Mendeley lists CC BY 4.0. Attribution: Vinh Truong Hoang (2020), HGM-4, Mendeley Data, V8.
- The associated article describes five volunteers photographed at four views, with backgrounds removed using Otsu thresholding. The archive is a 228,825,330-byte RAR with fixed SHA-256 recorded in the source manifest.
- macOS `bsdtar` lists the archive but cannot extract entries that use filters (`Parsing filters is unsupported`). A partial extraction is not trusted and was not converted or added to this repository.
- Decision: hold image mirroring and student distribution until full extraction, image review, participant/privacy review, and split design are completed.
