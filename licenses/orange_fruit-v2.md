## Educational fit and limits

The images can support a student prototype that explores visual categories and how backgrounds, lighting, or viewpoints affect sorting in a food-waste discussion. The dataset was captured in Bangladesh and does not represent all oranges or produce sold in Japan. Labels are dataset categories only; they do not establish freshness, edibility, food safety, or plant disease.
