## Intended educational use and limits

室内に残された紙・プラスチック・キャップ・殻を検出し、清掃箇所の確認を補助する. 床面ごみの検出候補。4,000 images, 6,968 boxes. Distant people occur in some frames; 50-image sample showed no clearly identifiable faces; this sample is not a privacy clearance. Local waste rules require human confirmation.
